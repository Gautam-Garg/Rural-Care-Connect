Connecting Every Villager to Primary Health Care

Rural Care Connect bypasses big-city metro hospital bias. We empower rural families and ASHA workers across Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, and Bihar to quickly locate Primary Health Centres (PHC), check doctor on duty, book free maternal care (JSY), track upcoming village vaccination drives, and access affordable medicines under PM Jan Aushadhi.
